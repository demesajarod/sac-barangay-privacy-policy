Request Account Deletion

Residents using the SAC Barangay Citizen App can submit a request to delete their account. Requests are reviewed by the barangay system administrator.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the SAC Barangay Citizen App.
  2. Log in to your resident account.
  3. Open the Settings screen.
  4. Select Request Account Deletion.
  5. Read the confirmation message.
  6. Tap Submit Request.
Submitting a request does not immediately delete your account. The request is sent to the administrator for review.

What Happens After Submission?

The administrator can identify accounts with pending deletion requests. Your account remains available until the request is processed.

The account deletion process is currently being finalized. Submission of a request should not be interpreted as confirmation that your personal information has already been erased.

Account Data and Retention

The SAC Barangay system may hold account information such as your name, email address, contact information, and records associated with barangay services.

The categories of information that will be permanently deleted, any information retained for legitimate administrative or legal purposes, and applicable retention periods are still being established.

This page will be updated when those policies and the associated deletion procedures are finalized.
