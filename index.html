Privacy Policy

SAC Barangay Citizen App

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SAC Barangay Citizen App collects, uses, stores, and protects information when residents use its barangay services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Account details, including name, email address, phone number, and registration information.
  • Login and authentication information, including information provided through Google Sign-In.
  • Concern reports, descriptions, categories, locations, and uploaded photo evidence.
  • Document requests and information submitted for barangay services.
  • Chat messages and other communications sent through the app.
  • Technical information needed for app operation, security, and notifications.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to register and verify accounts, process barangay requests, receive and manage concerns, communicate updates, provide notifications, maintain service security, and support the operation of the app.

3. Service Providers and Data Sharing

Authorized barangay personnel may access information as necessary to perform their assigned duties. The app uses technology services including a Spring Boot backend, database hosting, Supabase Storage, Firebase, and Google authentication services. These services may process information needed to deliver their functions. We do not sell residents' personal information.

4. Photos and Location Information

When you submit a concern with photo evidence or location information, that content is used to assess and manage the concern. Photo evidence is stored in private storage and retrieved through authenticated backend access for authorized personnel.

5. Data Security

We use authentication, role-based access controls, and other reasonable safeguards to protect personal information. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

Information is retained as necessary to provide barangay services, maintain appropriate records, and comply with applicable obligations. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information. Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

7. Notifications

The app may send notifications about concerns, document requests, announcements, and service updates. Notification delivery may involve Firebase services.

8. Children's Privacy

If information about minors is submitted through the app, it should be provided only with appropriate authority or consent and in accordance with applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions, data access, correction, or deletion requests, email demesajarod75@gmail.com.
